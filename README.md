# Shun-Cheng Wu

**Postdoctoral Researcher at Technical University of Munich (TUM) | Chief Technology Officer at VisualAIs**

I work on **3D computer vision**, with a focus on **scene reconstruction**, **3D scene understanding**, and **semantic scene graphs**. My current postdoctoral research at TUM's CAMP Chair investigates **3D foundation models for scene reconstruction**. At VisualAIs, I develop methods for realistic **3D rendering from smartphone captures**.

I completed my **PhD (Dr. rer. nat.) at TUM in March 2026**. Previously, I was a research intern at **NVIDIA Research (Taipei)**, working on prompt-based image editing using large language models. My background also includes research software engineering, SLAM, augmented reality, and technology entrepreneurship.

### Research interests

- 3D scene reconstruction and understanding
- 3D foundation models and neural rendering
- Semantic scene graphs and geometric learning
- Visual localization and SLAM
- Generative AI for visual content
